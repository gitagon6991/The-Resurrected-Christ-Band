WHO WE ARE

Revealing Christ Through Music

The Resurrected Christ Band is a gospel music ministry under Mount Olives Worship Sanctuary in Olekasasi B, Kajiado, Kenya. Founded in 2024, our mission is to reveal Jesus Christ to a dying world through worship, praise and music that uplifts, transforms and glorifies God.

OUR JOURNEY

Our Story

January 2024

Founded by Daisy Ivans with a vision to proclaim Christ through music.

February 2024

Aaron Katoo and Anthony Otieno joined, helping the ministry begin to grow.

2024–2025

Monthly rehearsals were held in Nairobi studios as the ministry continued to develop.

June 2025

The band found a spiritual home at Mount Olives Worship Sanctuary under Bishop Peter Mokaya and Rev. Rose Mokaya.

Today

The ministry continues to impact lives through worship events, outreach and praise experiences.

Mission

To become a global music ministry that glorifies Jesus and transforms lives through excellent worship.

Vision

To reveal Jesus Christ and glorify Him through music that uplifts and transforms the Body of Christ.

Core Purpose

Revealing Christ to a dying world while being a blessing to humanity.

Founder

Daisy Ivans

Founder • Vocalist • Keyboardist

Daisy Ivans is a gifted vocalist, keyboardist, entrepreneur and student at Africa Nazarene University. As the founder of The Resurrected Christ Band, established in January 2024, she is passionate about using music as a platform to proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ. Daisy is dedicated to raising a generation of worshippers, psalmists and ministers who faithfully serve God's purpose both locally and globally.

Drummer

Anthony Otieno is a passionate drummer committed to using music to glorify God and inspire people through worship. With a heart for excellence and faithful service, he brings energy, creativity and precision to every performance. Whether ministering during church services, concerts or community outreach events, Anthony believes drumming is more than rhythm. Saxophonist

Patrick Migici Mwangi

Gospel Saxophonist

Patrick Migici Mwangi is a passionate gospel saxophonist dedicated to glorifying God through the gift of music. With a heart for worship and a deep desire to minister to others, he uses the rich and expressive sound of the saxophone to create moments of praise, encouragement and spiritual reflection.

Whether serving during church services, gospel concerts or ministry events, Patrick believes that every melody should point people to Christ. His commitment to excellence and heartfelt worship inspires faith, brings hope and creates an atmosphere where people can experience God's presence.

Lead Guitar • Bass • Piano • Auxiliary Keys

Richard Wekesa is a devoted musician with a deep passion for Christian music and worship ministry. Gifted in playing the lead guitar, bass guitar, piano and auxiliary keys, he brings versatility and excellence to every worship experience.

As a committed member of The Resurrected Christ Band, he faithfully serves through his musical gifts while striving to glorify God through authentic worship.
