WHO WE ARE

Revealing Christ Through Music

The Resurrected Christ Band is a gospel music ministry under Mount Olives Worship Sanctuary in Olekasasi B, Kajiado, Kenya. Founded in 2024, our mission is to reveal Jesus Christ to a dying world through worship, praise and music that uplifts, transforms and glorifies God.

OUR JOURNEY

Our Story

January 2024

Founded by Daisy Ivans with a vision to proclaim Christ through music.

February 2024

Aaron Katoo and Anthony Otieno joined, helping the ministry begin to grow.

2024–2025

Monthly rehearsals were held in Nairobi studios as the ministry continued to develop.

June 2025

The band found a spiritual home at Mount Olives Worship Sanctuary under Bishop Peter Mokaya and Rev. Rose Mokaya.

Today

The ministry continues to impact lives through worship events, outreach and praise experiences.

Mission

To become a global music ministry that glorifies Jesus and transforms lives through excellent worship.

Vision

To reveal Jesus Christ and glorify Him through music that uplifts and transforms the Body of Christ.

Core Purpose

Revealing Christ to a dying world while being a blessing to humanity.
